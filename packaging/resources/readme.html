Getting started

  • New Software Instrument track → Instrument slot → AU Instruments → Catacomb → Catacomb.
  • Press play: the generative sequencers run, locked to the project's grid. MIDI notes (regions, Live Loops, a keyboard) trigger it and transpose the VCO.
  • Try the factory presets (the plugin header's preset menu, or Preset on the panel).
  • Patch bay: drag between jacks to patch; drag a cable out of an input to move or remove it; hover a jack to see what it does.

What we'd love to hear

  • Anything that sounds wrong, clicks, or is too quiet/loud — which preset or settings, and how.
  • Knobs whose range feels off (too much too soon, not enough at the top).
  • Crashes, silence, or the panel looking broken — a screenshot helps. The line at the top of the Sequencer section shows the version and what the plugin sees (audio, transport, MIDI, level).
  • Your Mac, macOS version, Logic/GarageBand version and the project's sample rate.

Known limits of this build

  • It isn't signed by an Apple developer yet, so macOS asks you to approve the installer once.
  • Mono voice (like the hardware it's modelled on); stereo output is the same on both sides.
