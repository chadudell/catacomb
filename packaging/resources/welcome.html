Catacomb @VERSION@ — tester build

Catacomb is a generative semi-modular synthesizer for Logic Pro and GarageBand: a sine VCO and a triangle MOD VCO with thru-zero FM, ring mod and noise, a wavefolder and a state-variable filter in parallel or series, two decay envelopes, two mutating 8-step sequencers and a 32-jack patch bay.

This installs the Catacomb Audio Unit. It needs macOS 11 or later (Apple Silicon or Intel).

Please quit Logic Pro and GarageBand before installing.
